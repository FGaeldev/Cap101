The Problem

Republic Act No. 12027 discontinued the use of mother tongue as the primary medium of instruction from Kindergarten to Grade 3, reverting to Filipino and English while retaining regional languages as auxiliary media. This may limit opportunities for young Aklanons to develop their Akeanon vocabulary through formal education.

Key Features

1Tap Akeanon words in dialogue to see their meanings
24 chapters, 41 quests across Kalibo landmarks
3In-game dictionary of discovered words
412 vocabulary mini-games and quizzes

Our Solution

An interactive RPG set in Kalibo that teaches Standard Akeanon through NPC conversations, contextual vocabulary discovery, quests, an in-game dictionary, and educational mini-games.

Target Users

  • Akeanon Learners – Students and individuals interested in learning Standard Akeanon vocabulary.
  • Educators – Teachers looking for supplementary language-learning materials.
  • Local Community – Akeanons interested in preserving and promoting their language.
